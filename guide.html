Local Discovery Editorial • 12 Locations

Enniscorthy & County Wexford: The Community Discovery Journal

Curated by Enniscorthy Indian Association for our families, healthcare professionals, new residents, and visiting friends. From historic riverside fortresses and 1798 battlefields in town, to coastal sand dunes, Victorian gardens, and wildlife reserves across sunny South East Ireland.

12 Standout Sites 6 Town Centre Walks 6 County Day Trips 100% Family Friendly Updated for 2026
1
Town Centre Landmark
0.2 km from Town Centre Castle Hill • Est. 1205

Enniscorthy Castle

Dominating the town skyline above the River Slaney, this Norman castle has witnessed over 800 years of turbulent Irish history. Visitors can climb to the battlements for panoramic 360-degree views across Enniscorthy's rooftops, Vinegar Hill, and the winding river below.

Family & Visitor Tip Excellent indoor exhibition exploring local Wexford crafts, the Norman invasion, and famous Enniscorthy designer Eileen Gray. Ideal on a rainy morning, with safe stairs for children and welcoming staff.
Rooftop Battlements River Slaney Views Norman Heritage Exhibition
Open daily 10:00 – 17:00 Google Maps Directions →
2
Panoramic Battlefield
1.6 km from Centre Historic 1798 Site

Vinegar Hill (Cnoc Fíodh na gCaor)

Vinegar Hill was the pivotal battleground and command post during the Wexford 1798 Rebellion. Today, this gentle volcanic hill offers one of the most stunning viewing points in County Wexford, looking out over the Slaney Valley, Mount Leinster, and the Blackstairs Mountains.

Family & Visitor Tip Paved parking lot available halfway up. The final grassy climb takes just 5 minutes and is great for kids and evening sunsets. Free 24/7 public access; bring a windbreaker jacket.
Free Entry Sunset Panorama Windmill Ruins Short Walk
Open 24 hours • Free Admission Google Maps Directions →
3
Interactive Heritage
0.8 km from Centre Parnell Road

The National 1798 Rebellion Centre

An award-winning national museum chronicling the dramatic 1798 uprising through immersive state-of-the-art 4D audiovisual presentations, battle dioramas, artifacts, and interactive touchscreen displays detailing Ireland’s struggle for independence.

Family & Visitor Tip Combo tickets are available covering both Enniscorthy Castle and the 1798 Centre at a discounted rate. Highly recommended for school-age children learning Irish history.
4D Battle Theatre Interactive Screens Gift Shop & Cafe Indoor Attraction
Tue – Sun 10:00 – 16:30 Google Maps Directions →
4
Gothic Revival Masterpiece
0.4 km from Centre Cathedral Street • Built 1843

St Aidan’s Cathedral

Designed by renowned Gothic Revival architect Augustus Welby Pugin (who also designed the interior of the Palace of Westminster and Big Ben in London), St Aidan's Cathedral is constructed from local dressed granite and features soaring arches and intricate stained glass windows.

Family & Visitor Tip Step inside for a moment of peaceful reflection and admire the hand-carved stone capitals and acoustic vaulting. Free to enter outside of service times. Respectful quiet is appreciated.
Pugin Architecture Granite Masonry Free Entry Town Centre
Open daily for visitors Google Maps Directions →
5
River Slaney Scenic Trail
Starts at Promenade 3.5 km Trail • Flat Paved

The Riverwalk Enniscorthy

A peaceful walking path winding along the wooded banks of the River Slaney south of Enniscorthy town. Known for its serene atmosphere, weeping willows, leaping salmon, and diverse birdlife including swans, herons, and kingfishers.

Family & Visitor Tip Completely flat and paved, making it ideal for pushchairs, jogging, and evening walks with elderly relatives. Parking available at the riverside promenade near Riverside Park Hotel.
Stroller Friendly Birdwatching Salmon River Dog Walking
Open year-round • Daylight hours Google Maps Directions →
6
Community Playground
0.7 km from Centre Abbey Square / Island Road

Kids Park Enniscorthy

A modern, fenced-in community playground equipped with safe climbing frames, swings, zip-lines, and sensory play equipment suitable for toddlers to pre-teens. Set beside open green lawns where families can relax on sunny afternoons.

Family & Visitor Tip Benches and picnic tables provided inside the enclosed play area. Close to coffee shops and bakeries in town, making it an easy 1–2 hour break for young kids before or after school.
Enclosed Safety Toddler Friendly Picnic Tables Free Community Facility
Open daily from 08:30 till dusk Google Maps Directions →
7
Outdoor Living Museum
19 km • 18 min drive Ferrycarrig, Wexford

Irish National Heritage Park

One of Ireland's premiere outdoor heritage attractions, covering 35 acres of native woodland and estuary marsh. Wander through 9,000 years of reconstructed history, featuring authentic Stone Age campsites, Ringforts, Crannógs on water, Viking shipyards, and Norman forts.

Family & Visitor Tip Allow 2.5 to 3 hours for a complete visit. Live falconry displays, archery workshops, and a woodland playground are great for kids. Beautiful café overlooking the River Slaney estuary.
35 Acres Woodland Crannog on Lake Falconry Birds Full Day Trip
Open daily 09:30 – 17:30 Google Maps Directions →
8
Family Animal Park
14 km • 14 min drive Clonroche, Co. Wexford

Secret Valley Wildlife Park

A 14-acre family-run rescue sanctuary and farm park set in scenic Clonroche countryside. Home to over 200 animals including meerkats, lemurs, alpacas, reptiles, wallabies, and farm animals with interactive keeper talks and bottle-feeding sessions.

Family & Visitor Tip Check feeding times at arrival! Kids can pet bunnies, feed goats, ride quad trains, and tackle the outdoor obstacle course. Free on-site parking and picnic barns.
Animal Feeding Pony Rides Indoor Play Barn Toddler Favorite
Open weekends & school holidays 10:00 – 17:00 Google Maps Directions →
9
Victorian Estate & Fairies
21 km • 20 min drive Ballyedmond, Gorey

Wells House & Gardens

A grand 17th-century Victorian Tudor-Gothic estate surrounded by 450 acres of historic gardens, formal terraces, woodland fairy trails, an animal farm, and an artisan craft courtyard. The mansion house itself offers guided living history tours.

Family & Visitor Tip The woodland 'Fairy Trail' and 'Mogue's Walk' feature charming wooden sculptures that delight young children. High ropes adventure and archery available for older youth.
Fairy Woodland Trail Mansion Tours Animal Corner Coffee Courtyard
Open daily 10:00 – 17:00 Google Maps Directions →
10
Castle Lakes & Museum
28 km • 25 min drive Murntown, Wexford

Johnstown Castle Estate & Gardens

A Gothic Revival castle set beside three tranquil ornamental lakes teeming with peacocks, swans, and rare champion trees. The estate houses the Irish Agricultural Museum, Ireland's largest transport and rural heritage collection, and scenic lakeside pathways.

Family & Visitor Tip Book the castle servant tunnel tour — kids love walking through the 86-metre underground brick servant passageway. Excellent visitor centre café and playground on-site.
Servant Tunnels Lake Walks Irish Agri Museum Peacock Gardens
Open 7 days 09:00 – 17:30 Google Maps Directions →
11
Blue Flag Sand Dunes
24 km • 24 min drive Curracloe, Co. Wexford

Curracloe Beach & Raven Wood

Famous worldwide as the opening landing location in the film Saving Private Ryan, Curracloe Beach stretches for miles of soft golden sand backed by wild dune systems. Directly adjacent is the Raven Nature Reserve, a pine forest trail where you can spot red squirrels.

Family & Visitor Tip Blue Flag designated beach with lifeguards on duty during summer months. Surf schools operate nearby, and the forest trail offers shelter if the beach is windy.
Blue Flag Waters Miles of Golden Sand Pine Forest Walks Summer Swimming
Public beach • Free access year-round Google Maps Directions →
12
Medieval Capital of Leinster
11 km • 10 min drive Ferns, North Wexford

Ferns Castle & Medieval Monasteries

Once the ancient capital of Leinster, historic Ferns contains an impressive 13th-century Norman castle with a rare circular private chapel on the second floor, St Mary's Augustinian Abbey, and ancient high crosses.

Family & Visitor Tip The OPW visitor centre houses the hand-stitched Ferns Tapestry detailing the arrival of the Normans. Free entry into the castle grounds; combined easily with a short drive to Gorey.
Ancient Capital Norman Chapel OPW Tapestry 10 Min from Town
Open Easter to autumn 10:00 – 17:00 Google Maps Directions →
