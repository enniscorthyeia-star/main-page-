About Our Association

Together, We Celebrate. Together, We Connect. Together, We Grow.

Enniscorthy Indian Association (EIA) is a community organisation established to bring together Indian families, individuals and friends living in Enniscorthy and the surrounding areas of County Wexford.

EIA is committed to celebrating the rich cultural heritage of India while creating meaningful connections with the wider Irish community. Our aim is to provide a welcoming and inclusive environment where people from different backgrounds can come together, share traditions, build friendships and create lasting memories.

Our Vision

“Our vision is to create a strong, united and inclusive community where Indian culture and traditions can be proudly celebrated while embracing the diversity and values of Ireland.”

Throughout the year, EIA organises a wide range of cultural, social, sporting and family-oriented activities. Our celebrations include major Indian festivals such as Onam, Vishu and Diwali, alongside community gatherings, family events and multicultural programmes.

Core Pillars

Empowering Culture, Sports & Community

Our four pillars define how EIA enriches family life and cross-cultural friendship in County Wexford.

Culture & Arts

Music, dance, and traditional performances helping young generations cherish heritage.

Sports & Activities

EIA Sports and Arts Club (PPN-00677) — EIA Summer Badminton / Tennis Championship, inter-county cricket, athletics, and active recreation for children and adults alike.

Local Discovery • 12 Attractions

Explore Enniscorthy & County Wexford

A curated guide of 12 standout landmarks and memorable family day trip destinations recommended for our community and visitors.

1
Town Centre

Enniscorthy Castle

Right in the town centre. A great place to learn about Enniscorthy’s history and enjoy views over the River Slaney.

View on Map →
2
Panoramic Views

Vinegar Hill

Historic 1798 battlefield with panoramic views of Enniscorthy. A particularly nice short walk for families and morning strolls.

View on Map →
3
Interactive Heritage

The National 1798 Rebellion Centre

Interactive exhibitions about the 1798 rebellion. Close to Enniscorthy Castle, so both can easily be combined in one visit.

View on Map →
4
Gothic Revival

St Aidan’s Cathedral

Beautiful historic cathedral in the centre of Enniscorthy designed by renowned architect Augustus Welby Pugin.

View on Map →
5
River Slaney

The Riverwalk Enniscorthy

A pleasant option for a relaxed family walk along the River Slaney with scenic views, flora, and birdlife.

View on Map →
6
Family Playground

Kids Park

Good option if you’re visiting with younger children. Fully equipped safe play areas, slides, swings, and green open lawns.

View on Map →
7
Ferrycarrig

Irish National Heritage Park

One of the best family attractions in the area, taking visitors through Ireland’s history with reconstructed settlements and monuments.

View on Map →
8
Clonroche

Secret Valley Wildlife Park

A good choice for children and families who enjoy animals and outdoor exotic animal rescue encounters.

View on Map →
9
Near Gorey

Wells House & Gardens

Historic Victorian house, enchanting gardens, fairy woodland walks, craft courtyard, and engaging family activities.

View on Map →
10
Wexford

Johnstown Castle Estate & Gardens

Beautiful ornamental gardens, castle surroundings, lake walks, and museum; excellent for a full family day out.

View on Map →
11
Wexford Coast

Curracloe Beach

A beautiful long sandy beach around the Wexford coast, particularly good in summer for swimming, beach games, and dunes.

View on Map →
12
North Wexford

Ferns Castle & Medieval Ferns

A lovely historical outing north of Enniscorthy. Ferns Castle is one of the area’s standout historic medieval attractions.

Community Welfare

Member Support & Services

We are here to support every member and family throughout their life and journey in Enniscorthy.

Newcomer Support

Practical integration guidance for newly arriving healthcare staff and families relocating to Wexford.

  • PPS & IRP Registration Guidance
  • Local GP & Healthcare Clinics
  • School & Crèche Admissions
  • Wexford County Council Services

Language & Culture

Weekend classes helping children develop bilingual fluency and pride in Indian classical arts.

  • Malayalam & Indian Language Lessons
  • Classical Dance & Bharatanatyam
  • Carnatic Vocal Music Sessions
  • Heritage Storytelling Workshops

Community Welfare

A mutual-aid network assisting members during times of illness, bereavement, or family emergencies.

  • Emergency Volunteer Support Team
  • Hospital Visits & Meal Assistance
  • Consular & Embassy Assistance
  • Senior Citizens Community Circle

Sports & Youth Hub

Engaging youth through competitive sports, healthy active lifestyles, and university mentorship.

  • EIA Cricket Club & Tournaments
  • Badminton & Athletics Meets
  • Career & College Guidance
  • Youth Leadership Certificates
