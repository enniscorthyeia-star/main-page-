Our Story

A community connected by where we come from - and where we're going.

The Enniscorthy Indian Association (Kerala Wing) was founded with a simple conviction: that the ties of language, culture, and shared memory deserve a place to grow - even far from home.

What began as a handful of families gathering for Onam has become a thriving community of hundreds across County Wexford - organising festivals, preserving language, supporting newcomers, and building friendships that span generations.

We are not just an association. We are a living, breathing piece of Kerala in Ireland - carrying forward the warmth, the food, the stories, and the spirit that make our heritage worth celebrating.

Our Heritage

The living traditions of Kerala

From the rhythms of Kathakali to the flavours of a sadya - our culture is not a museum piece. It's something we practice, share, and pass on.

Arts & Heritage

Centuries of classical mastery - Kathakali, mural art, Sanskrit theatre, and ancient storytelling traditions.

Grand Festivals

Onam, Vishu, Pooram beats, and celebrations uniting families across Wexford with pookalam and feasts.

Sacred Traditions

The warmth of the Nilavilakku lamp, timeless customs, auspicious beginnings, and shared communal dining.

Music & Dance

Theyyam fire dances, Mohiniyattam grace, Chenda Melam thunder, and youth performances.

Language & Lore

Keeping Malayalam alive across generations through weekend classes, literature, poetry, and storytelling.
