About Our Community

A Warm Welcome on the Banks of River Slaney

Connecting Heritage with Modern Irish Community Life

Enniscorthy Indian Association (EIA) is a community organisation established to bring together Indian families, individuals and friends living in Enniscorthy and the surrounding areas of County Wexford.

EIA is committed to celebrating the rich cultural heritage of India while creating meaningful connections with the wider Irish community. Our aim is to provide a welcoming and inclusive environment where people from different backgrounds can come together, share traditions, build friendships and create lasting memories.

Our Vision

“Our vision is to create a strong, united and inclusive community where Indian culture and traditions can be proudly celebrated while embracing the diversity and values of Ireland.”

Throughout the year, EIA organises a wide range of cultural, social, sporting and family-oriented activities. Our celebrations include major Indian festivals such as Onam, Vishu and Diwali, alongside community gatherings, family events and multicultural programmes.

Culture & Arts

We encourage children, young people and adults to participate in Indian music, dance, traditional arts and cultural performances, fostering creativity and confidence.

Sports & Activities

EIA promotes an active and healthy community through sporting and recreational activities, including cricket championships, badminton, and family fitness days.

Community & Inclusion

Community is at the heart of EIA. We actively encourage volunteering, cooperation and welcome people of all nationalities and backgrounds into our events.

Supporting Next Gen

We provide opportunities for young people to participate in artistic, sporting and social activities, helping them appreciate both their Indian heritage and Irish community.

Explore Enniscorthy & County Wexford

12 wonderful landmarks and family day trip destinations recommended for our community and visitors.

1
Town Centre

Enniscorthy Castle

Right in the town centre. A great place to learn about Enniscorthy’s history and enjoy views over the River Slaney.

2
Panoramic Walk

Vinegar Hill

Historic 1798 battlefield with panoramic views of Enniscorthy. A particularly nice short walk for families and morning strolls.

View on Map
3
Interactive Heritage

The National 1798 Rebellion Centre

Interactive exhibitions about the 1798 rebellion. It’s close to Enniscorthy Castle, so both can easily be combined in one visit.

View on Map
4
Architecture

St Aidan’s Cathedral

Beautiful historic cathedral in the centre of Enniscorthy designed by famous architect Augustus Pugin.

View on Map
5
Nature & Family

The Riverwalk Enniscorthy

A pleasant option for a relaxed family walk along the River Slaney with scenic views, flora, and birdlife.

View on Map

Family Play

Kids Park

Good option if you’re visiting with younger children. Fully equipped playground with safe playing areas and park benches.

View on Map

Ferrycarrig

Irish National Heritage Park

One of the best family attractions in the area, taking visitors through Ireland’s history with reconstructed settlements and monuments.

View on Map
8
Clonroche

Secret Valley Wildlife Park

A good choice for children and families who enjoy animals and outdoor rescue rehabilitation experiences.

View on Map

Near Gorey

Wells House & Gardens

Historic house, enchanting Victorian gardens, fairy trails, craft courtyard and wonderful family activities.

Wexford

Johnstown Castle Estate & Gardens

Beautiful gardens, castle surroundings, lake walks and Irish Agricultural Museum; excellent for a full family day out.

A lovely historical outing north of Enniscorthy. Ferns Castle is one of the area’s standout historic medieval attractions.

Support & Welfare

Community Services & Member Support

Providing guidance, integration support, and assistance for all Indian residents and families settling in Enniscorthy.

Newcomer Integration

Guidance for newly arriving families in Enniscorthy and County Wexford with practical relocation advice.

  • PPS Number & IRP Registration Advice
  • Local GP & Health Centre Guidance
  • School & Crèche Admission Information
  • Local Wexford Council Services

Culture & Language Classes

Helping our children maintain bilingual skills and deep connection with their Indian roots.

  • Malayalam & Indian Language Workshops
  • Traditional Indian Classical Dance
  • Carnatic & Devotional Music Sessions
  • Youth Heritage Storytelling Club

Community Welfare

A caring mutual-aid network supporting members during times of illness, emergencies, or bereavement.

  • Senior Citizen Community Visits
