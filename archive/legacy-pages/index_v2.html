Who We Are

Two homelands, one hearth.

Building bridges of culture, community, and care in Enniscorthy and across Co. Wexford.

Welcome to EIA

Founded by Kerala and wider Indian expatriate families in Enniscorthy, the Enniscorthy Indian Association has grown into a vibrant cultural hub. From traditional grand Onam Sadyas on banana leaves to Diwali illumination events at the Athletic Grounds, we nurture heritage for our children while actively participating in local Wexford life.

Whether you are a new arrival looking for local support or a long-time resident seeking connection, our doors and hearts are always open.

Our Initiatives

Culture, care, and a foothold in local life.

Festivals & Gatherings

Celebrating Onam, Diwali, Vishu, Christmas and Eid with traditional music, dance, and food.

Classes for the Next Gen

Malayalam language lessons, classical Indian dance (Bharatanatyam), and music classes for children.

Education & New Arrivals

Guidance for nurses, doctors, IT professionals, and families moving to Enniscorthy and Co. Wexford.

Standing With Enniscorthy

Active participation in St. Patrick’s Day parades, Tidy Towns initiatives, and local civic projects.
