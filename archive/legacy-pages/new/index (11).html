About us

Rooted locally, open to everyone

RCC exists to help neighbours find each other. We host seasonal gatherings, share practical know-how for settling in, and give both long-time residents and newcomers a place to belong.

  • ✓ Neighbourly support
  • ✓ Cultural exchange
  • ✓ Openness and respect
  • ✓ Volunteer spirit
  • ✓ Honest, transparent running

What we do

Everyday ways we show up

🎉

Host seasonal gatherings and socials

Monthly meet-ups, festival celebrations, and casual potlucks that bring the street together.

📚

Run a settling-in guide for newcomers

Practical help with paperwork, schools, and local services for families new to the area.

🎓

Offer tutoring and small grants

Homework help and modest bursaries for students in member households.

🤝

Pair members with a local buddy

A friendly contact for anyone's first few months in Riverside.
