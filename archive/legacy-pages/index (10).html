About Us

Two homelands,
one hearth.

The Enniscorthy Indian Association began around a handful of kitchen tables and has grown into the gathering point for Indian families across Enniscorthy and the wider Wexford area. We keep our festivals, our food, and our languages alive — while building real roots in the town we now call home.

From Onam sadyas laid out on banana leaves to marching in Enniscorthy's own St Patrick's Day parade, we hold both traditions close, and pass them on to the next generation.
