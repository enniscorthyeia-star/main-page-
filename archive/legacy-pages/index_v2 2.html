About the Association

A hearth kept
on foreign ground.

The Enniscorthy Indian Association began around a handful of kitchen tables and has grown into the gathering point for Indian families across Wexford. We keep our festivals, our food and our languages alive, while building real roots in the town we now call home.

From Onam sadyas laid out on banana leaves to marching in Enniscorthy's own St Patrick's Day parade, we hold both traditions close — and we pass them on.

No membership is needed for your first Diwali evening with us. Just come.

What We Do

Culture, care, and a foothold in local life.

Festivals & gatherings

Onam, Diwali, Holi, and Republic and Independence Day — marked together, every year, with the whole community around the table.

Language for the next generation

Weekend classes in Malayalam, Hindi and Tamil, alongside classical dance and music for children raised in Wexford.

Support for new arrivals

Tutoring, mentoring and a welcome pack for families just settling into Enniscorthy — the practical kind of help, early on.

Standing with Enniscorthy

A presence at the Strawberry Fair, the Rackard Cup, and the town's own St Patrick's Day parade — this is home too.
