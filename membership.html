Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Enniscorthy Indian Association.

Who can join the EIA?

Membership is open to anyone of Indian heritage living in Enniscorthy and County Wexford, as well as friends, families, and supporters from all backgrounds through our Associate Tier.

How do I complete the membership payment?

Once you submit your application above, you will receive an instant email with payment details via Revolut, Irish Bank Transfer (IBAN), or cash payment to any Executive Committee member.

What does the membership cover?

Membership helps fund community cultural festivals, sports tournaments, children's Malayalam and arts classes, newcomer relocation support, and emergency community welfare.

How long is my membership valid?

Memberships run annually for the 2026 calendar year and are renewed each year ahead of the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
